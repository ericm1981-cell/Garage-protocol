Design Rules
1 — No pattern collision
The WOD never repeats the day's heavy lift pattern, and never repeats yesterday's WOD pattern. 48–72 hrs between heavy exposures of the same movement.
2 — Bike is the Zone 2 engine
Every WOD has protected bike intervals. Target 138–151 bpm — conversational pace. If you're breathing hard, slow down. That's the whole point.
3 — Heavy lift first, always
Strength before conditioning. Never do heavy Olympic lifts fatigued.

8-Week Progression
Weeks 1–2
Find working weights. Form over load. Complete every session. Learn the bike pace.
Weeks 3–4
Add load. Main lifts heavy by set four. Zone 2 should be 15+ min per session.
Weeks 5–6
Beat week 1–2 WOD scores. Lifts meaningfully heavier. Zone 2 at 20+ min.
Weeks 7–8
Full intensity. Week 8 retests week 1 WODs — that's the proof.
