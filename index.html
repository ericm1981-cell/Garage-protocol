Design Rules
1 — No pattern collision
The WOD never repeats the day's heavy lift pattern, and never repeats yesterday's WOD pattern. 48–72 hrs between heavy exposures of the same movement.
2 — Bike is the Zone 2 engine
Every WOD has protected bike intervals. Target 138–151 bpm — conversational pace. If you're breathing hard, slow down. That's the whole point.
3 — Heavy lift first, always
Strength before conditioning. Never do heavy Olympic lifts fatigued.

The Strength Build — % Based
Week 1 — TEST
Work up to a heavy 5-rep max on Deadlift, Back Squat, Strict Press. Enter each in the lift card. Every future week calculates off these numbers.
Weeks 2–4 — VOLUME
75% → 80% → 85% of your 5RM. Build the base with clean sets. 5x5 then 4x4.
Week 5 — DELOAD
65%. Deliberate recovery. Move fast and easy so you can build again heavier.
Weeks 6–7 — INTENSITY / PEAK
88% triples, then build to a heavy double at ~92%. Strength gets expressed.
Week 8 — RETEST
Find a NEW 5-rep max. Beat week 1. That number seeds your next 8-week block.
Each Session Has

2 — Accessory lift

3 — CrossFit WOD
Real metcons — benchmarks, barbell cycling, gymnastics skill, bike for Zone 2.
